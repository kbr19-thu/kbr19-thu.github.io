In **May 2026**, I co-founded [**Autodemia**](https://autodemia.com) and served as its **CEO**, embarking on an entrepreneurial journey to build trustworthy, programmable AI infrastructure for autonomous scientific research and engineering.

At Autodemia, we integrate multi-agent collaboration architectures, domain knowledge bases, compute scheduling, and multi-model routing into a unified system for scientific discovery, featuring core products such as:

- **AutoRes**: A flagship AI-autonomous research platform that orchestrates agents across literature review, hypothesis formulation, experiment execution, data analysis, and scientific reporting.
- **FigShot**: An AI data analysis and scientific visualization assistant that transforms raw experimental data and sketches into publication-ready figures.
